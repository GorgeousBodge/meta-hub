Meta Deck

Privacy policy and terms of use · last updated 28 October 2026

Meta Deck is a personal, non-commercial project: a set of web apps for one person's smart glasses. It is not offered to the public, and only its owner can sign in.

Privacy policy

When the owner connects a Gmail or Outlook.com account, Meta Deck uses Google and Microsoft permissions to:

  • list recent inbox messages and unread counts, open messages, and mark them as read;
  • send replies the owner has written or chosen and confirmed;
  • read calendar events (read-only).

Sign-in tokens are stored encrypted on the app's server. Message contents are fetched when needed and are not stored, except a short AI summary of a message, kept encrypted for up to seven days. To write summaries and suggested replies, the text of an opened message may be sent to Anthropic's Claude API, which does not use it to train models.

Data is never sold, shared for advertising, or used for any purpose other than showing the owner their own mail and calendar. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Disconnecting an account on the app's connect page deletes its stored sign-in. Access can also be removed at any time in your Google or Microsoft account security settings.

Terms of use

Meta Deck is provided as-is, for the owner's personal use only, with no warranty. No one else may use it.
